# Services

Source: Kualta Agency - Business Rules (Team Greek AI, Nov 8 2026). Kualta Agency is a digital marketing agency. Kualta offers only these listed services: social media management, paid ads, SEO, content creation, branding, and website design. Anything else needs management approval.

All rates are starting prices in Philippine pesos (PHP), VAT-exclusive. Final prices come from a written quotation after a free consultation.

## Social media management
Starting rate: ₱15,000 per month.
Includes: 2 platforms, 12 posts per month, captions, community replies, and a monthly report.
Turnaround: starts 7 business days after signing.

## Content creation
Starting rate: ₱8,000 per month.
Includes: 12 graphics or 4 short videos per month.
Turnaround: 5 business days per batch.

## Paid ads management
Starting rate: ₱10,000 per month plus ad spend.
Includes: Meta or Google Ads setup, targeting, weekly optimization, and a monthly report.
Turnaround: ads live within 5 business days.
Paid-ad budgets are paid by the client directly to the ad platform and are separate from Kualta's management fee.

## SEO
Starting rate: ₱12,000 per month.
Includes: site audit, keyword plan, on-page fixes, and 2 blog posts per month.
Turnaround: first report after 30 days.

## Branding package
Starting rate: ₱25,000 one-time.
Includes: logo, color palette, typography, and a brand guide (PDF).
Turnaround: 15 business days.

## Website design
Starting rate: ₱35,000 one-time.
Includes: WordPress site up to 5 pages, mobile-ready, contact form, and basic SEO.
Turnaround: 20 to 30 business days.

## Free consultation
A free initial consultation of up to 30 minutes, online via Google Meet, is offered before any quotation.

## Business hours
Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM (Philippine time). Closed weekends and public holidays. A staff member replies to new inquiries within 1 business day.
