# Privacy


## Privacy notice
Kualta Agency collects your name, email, phone number and business name only to reply to your inquiry and schedule a consultation. We follow the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), keep chat records for up to 12 months, and never sell or share your data. To view, correct or delete your data, reply 'DELETE MY DATA' or ask our team.

## Client data
Client and lead data is handled under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173): collected for a stated purpose, kept only as long as needed, and never sold or shared outside the project.
