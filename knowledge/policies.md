# Policies


## How an engagement works
Every client engagement follows one path: inquiry, consultation, quotation, signed contract and deposit, delivery, final payment, and handover.

## Quotations
Final prices are given only in a written quotation after consultation. Published rates are starting prices, not final. A quotation is valid for 15 calendar days from issue.

## Contract
No work starts until the client signs the service agreement and pays the deposit.

## Payment
A 50% non-refundable deposit is due on signing; the balance is due before final files or account access are handed over. Monthly retainers (for example social media management) are billed in advance and due by the 5th of each month. Accounts unpaid 15 days past due are paused until settled.

## Delivery and timelines
Each project has a written timeline. Delays caused by late client materials or approvals extend the deadline by the same number of days.

## Revisions
Each deliverable includes 2 revision rounds. Extra rounds cost ₱1,500 each; scope changes are quoted separately.

## Approval
Client approval must be given in writing (email or project channel). No reply within 5 business days counts as approved.

## Results
Kualta does not guarantee specific results (followers, rankings, sales); it commits only to agreed deliverables and reporting.

## Reporting
Retainer clients receive a monthly performance report.

## Cancellation
Retainers can be cancelled with 30 days' written notice. One-off projects cancelled mid-work are billed for work completed; the deposit is not refunded.

## Ownership
Final files and IP transfer to the client only after full payment. Kualta may show the work in its portfolio unless the client opts out in writing.

## Account access
Client logins (social media, website, ad accounts) are stored only in the agency's password manager and removed when the engagement ends.

## Complaints
Complaints are acknowledged within 1 business day and escalated to the account manager.
